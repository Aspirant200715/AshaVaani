The Problem Solved

40% of an ASHA worker's day is lost to paper registers.

Privacy Policy • Sovereign Data Governance

1. Sovereign Data Residency: All audio streaming, transcript processing, and clinical extraction occur strictly on Indian domestic infrastructure in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

2. Synthetic & Anonymized Clinical Data: All demo scenarios contain zero personally identifiable information (PII). In live field environments, patient records are tokenized to anonymized ABHA addresses.

3. Zero Model Retention Policy: Spoken audio sent to Gnani AI's Speech APIs is processed ephemerally in memory and is never used for general model training without explicit consent.

4. ABDM Compliance: Clinical records are formatted according to the FHIR R4 specification approved by the National Health Authority (NHA).

Terms of Service & Clinical Disclaimer

1. Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS): AshaVaani is an ambient voice copilot designed to assist accredited healthcare workers. It is not an autonomous medical diagnostic device.

2. Healthcare Worker Oversight: A certified healthcare professional (ASHA, ANM, or Medical Officer) must always verify transcribed vital signs, triage categorizations, and medication dosages.

3. Open Source Research License: This prototype is created for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge under open-source evaluation terms.

Security & Cryptographic Architecture

TLS 1.3 IN-TRANSIT: All audio packets streamed to Gnani Prisma v3.0 endpoints are encrypted using AES-256-GCM.

EPHEMERAL VOICE BUFFERS: Audio buffers in memory are wiped immediately after transcription.

ABDM ARTHA PROTOCOL: ABDM FHIR JSON bundles utilize cryptographic digital signatures conforming to National Health Authority (NHA) specifications.
