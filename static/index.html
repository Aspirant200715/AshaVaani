The Problem Solved

40% of an ASHA worker's day is lost to paper registers.

Over 1 million community health workers across 600,000 villages are forced to translate rapid, code-mixed patient complaints into rigid portal forms while holding crying infants or traveling over village terrain.

Conventional speech models fail because real calls travel over 8kHz telephone networks distorted by traffic, livestock, and dialect slang. AshaVaani eliminates the administrative burden, returning 2.5 hours every single day back to patient care.

Privacy Policy • Sovereign Data Governance

1. Sovereign Data Residency: All audio streaming, transcript processing, and clinical extraction occur strictly on Indian domestic infrastructure in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

2. Synthetic & Anonymized Clinical Data: All demo scenarios contain zero personally identifiable information (PII). In live field environments, patient records are tokenized to anonymized ABHA addresses.

3. Zero Model Retention Policy: Spoken audio sent to Gnani AI's Speech APIs is processed ephemerally in memory and is never used for general model training without explicit consent.

4. ABDM Compliance: Clinical records are formatted according to the FHIR R4 specification approved by the National Health Authority (NHA).

Terms of Service

AshaVaani is an ambient clinical copilot created to assist accredited health workers (ASHA, ANM, and CHOs). It does not replace clinical judgment by a licensed medical practitioner.

All emergency red-flag triages must be corroborated by direct physical evaluation and immediate dispatch of 108 Ambulance services.

Security Architecture

TLS 1.3 In-Transit: All client-server-Gnani network transactions are encrypted using TLS 1.3.

Sandboxed SQLite: Consultation logs are stored locally within the container without external network exposure.

Audit Trail: Every voice inference carries an immutable request ID resolving back to the sovereign stack.
