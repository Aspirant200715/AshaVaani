Engineered for the reality of Indian healthcare.

Foreign clinical models fail on Indian telephony, village acoustic noise, and regional dialects. AshaVaani is built natively on Gnani AI's 14M+ hours of Indian speech data.

#NoiseCanceller Award

8kHz Telephony Acoustic Filtering

Trained directly on low-bandwidth PSTN and GSM phone audio. Filters crying infants in village clinics, traffic roar, and cell tower static where baseline Whisper models drop critical clinical words.

Patients don't speak pure textbook Hindi. Prisma v3.0 seamlessly handles code-mixing like *"Baby ko do din se high fever aur loose motion hai"* without switching modes or dropping medical nouns.

Privacy Policy • Sovereign Data Governance

1. Sovereign Data Residency: All audio streaming, transcript processing, and medical entity extraction strictly occur on Indian domestic servers in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. No audio or health data is ever transmitted across international borders.

2. Synthetic & Anonymized Health Data: By default, demonstration audio clips contain zero personally identifiable information (PII). In live field operations, patient identities are tokenized against anonymized ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) identifiers.

3. Zero Model Retention Policy: Spoken audio recordings sent to the Gnani AI Speech APIs are processed ephemerally in memory and are not used for public model training without explicit institutional consent.

4. ABDM Compliance: Structured health records are generated according to the FHIR R4 profile standard authorized by the National Health Authority (NHA) of India.

Terms of Service

AshaVaani is an ambient clinical copilot designed to assist accredited healthcare professionals (ASHA, ANM, and PHC Medical Officers). It is not a substitute for formal medical diagnosis by a licensed physician.

All emergency red-flag triages must be corroborated by direct physical evaluation and immediate emergency dispatch (108 Ambulance service).

Security Architecture

Encryption in Transit: All communications between client, AshaVaani API, and Gnani Speech engines use TLS 1.3 encryption.

Local Encryption at Rest: Consultation logs in SQLite are restricted to the local operating container with zero external attack surfaces.

RBAC Ready: Designed to interface with hospital management systems (HMIS) with role-based access for CHOs and ASHA facilitators.
